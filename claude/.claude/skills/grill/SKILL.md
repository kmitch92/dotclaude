---
name: grill
description: Interview to stress-test a plan or architecture before you build it
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview the user about this plan or architecture until reaching shared understanding, walking down each branch of the architecture tree, and resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. Give a recommended answer with each question.

Give the user one question at a time, and wait for the answer before you continue, since many questions at one time confuse the user.

If the codebase can answer a question, explore it instead of giving that question to the user.

Do not act on the plan until you and the user share an understanding of it.
</content>
